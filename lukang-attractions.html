Lukang Mazu Temple

Lukang Tianhou Temple is one of the oldest and most important Mazu temples in Taiwan, with over 400 years of history. It houses a rare Mazu statue brought from Meizhou, making it a significant spiritual site.

Known for its exquisite wood and stone carvings, the temple is not only a place of worship but also a masterpiece of traditional Taiwanese architecture. Today, it remains the cultural and spiritual heart of Lukang, attracting visitors and pilgrims from around the world.

Breast Touching Alley

At just 70cm wide, this 200-year-old alley was designed to block freezing sea winds. Its cheeky name comes from the inevitable "close encounters" when two people pass each other. A legendary spot for a fun "tight squeeze" photo!

A must-visit for a unique perspective on old-town living.

Osmanthus Alley Artist Village

Lukang Artist Village is a creative art village in Lukang, transformed from Japanese-era dormitories.

It features resident artists, handmade crafts, colorful murals, and unique cultural products, making it a charming spot for visitors.

Lukang Folk Arts Museum

Lukang Folk Arts Museum in Changhua, Taiwan, is a unique cultural attraction featuring European-style architecture and traditional Taiwanese houses. Originally the residence of a wealthy merchant.

it now displays historical artifacts from the Qing Dynasty to early modern Taiwan. This museum offers visitors a rare 'glimpse into Lukang’s rich history, architecture, and cultural heritage.

Taiwan Glass Temple , Lukang

Taiwan Glass Temple in Lukang is a unique Mazu temple built entirely with glass. Known for its stunning architecture and beautiful night lighting,

it combines traditional Taiwanese religion with modern design, making it a must-visit attraction in Changhua, Taiwan.
