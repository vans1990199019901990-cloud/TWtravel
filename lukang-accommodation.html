Indigo Hotel Lukang

Opening in 2028, Hotel Indigo Lukang is the first international 5-star hotel in Changhua. Its design blends red-brick heritage with "sailing boat" motifs. Located near historic temples, it offers 115 rooms, an indoor pool, and local-inspired dining, providing a luxurious gateway to Lukang’s ancient culture.
